XVIII The Moon
Major Arcana · XVIII

The Moon

Intuition · uncertainty · dreams · things not yet clear

Upright meaning

The Moon shows a path lit only by moonlight: you can see the way, but not every detail. It speaks of strong feelings, dreams and worries that make a situation look bigger or stranger than it is.

In a reading it asks you to slow down. Notice what you feel, then check the facts before you decide. Not everything is clear yet — and that is fine.

The Moon reversed

Reversed, the fog starts to lift. A fear loses its grip, a confusing situation becomes easier to read, or you finally say out loud what has been bothering you.

It can also mean ignoring your intuition for too long. The advice: trust the clearer picture you now have.
